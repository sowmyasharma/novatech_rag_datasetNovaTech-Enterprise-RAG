# Support SLA

## Scope
This policy applies to Global NovaTech staff and authorized stakeholders. Support SLA is maintained by SALES Operations.

## Policy requirement
P1 response 30 minutes; P2 four hours; P3 one business day; Enterprise P1 24/7.

## Implementation steps
1. Log the request, business purpose, owner, and applicable region. 2. Verify the current policy version and eligibility. 3. Obtain the designated approval before execution. 4. Record the outcome and evidence in the department tracking system. 5. Escalate exceptions to SALES Operations.

## Worked example
A staff member asks about support sla. The reviewer checks the rule above, validates employee scope, records approvals, and follows the related procedure in PROD-001. Do not use superseded guidance for current requests.

## Controls and exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from SALES Operations. Confidential or Restricted material must only be retrieved for entitled roles. The owning team reviews the policy annually.

## Cross-department handoff
