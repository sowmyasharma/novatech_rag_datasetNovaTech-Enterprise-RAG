# Retention Schedule

## Scope
This policy applies to Global NovaTech staff and authorized stakeholders. Retention Schedule is maintained by LEGAL Operations.

## Policy requirement
Contracts retained seven years after termination; personnel files six years after separation, unless legal hold.

## Implementation steps
1. Log the request, business purpose, owner, and applicable region. 2. Verify the current policy version and eligibility. 3. Obtain the designated approval before execution. 4. Record the outcome and evidence in the department tracking system. 5. Escalate exceptions to LEGAL Operations.

## Worked example
A staff member asks about retention schedule. The reviewer checks the rule above, validates employee scope, records approvals, and follows the related procedure in IT-004. Do not use superseded guidance for current requests.

## Controls and exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from LEGAL Operations. Confidential or Restricted material must only be retrieved for entitled roles. The owning team reviews the policy annually.

## Cross-department handoff
For overlapping questions, consult IT-004. If policies disagree, use the active version effective on the relevant date and escalate unresolved conflicts to the owners.
