# Disaster Recovery

## Scope
This policy applies to Global NovaTech staff and authorized stakeholders. Disaster Recovery is maintained by ENG Operations.

## Policy requirement
Tier-1 RTO four hours and RPO one hour; twice-yearly DR simulation.

## Implementation steps
1. Log the request, business purpose, owner, and applicable region. 2. Verify the current policy version and eligibility. 3. Obtain the designated approval before execution. 4. Record the outcome and evidence in the department tracking system. 5. Escalate exceptions to ENG Operations.

## Worked example
A staff member asks about disaster recovery. The reviewer checks the rule above, validates employee scope, records approvals, and follows the related procedure in PROD-005. Do not use superseded guidance for current requests.

## Controls and exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from ENG Operations. Confidential or Restricted material must only be retrieved for entitled roles. The owning team reviews the policy annually.
