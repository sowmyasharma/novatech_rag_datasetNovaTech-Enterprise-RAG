# Onboarding Checklist

## Scope
This policy applies to US NovaTech staff and authorized stakeholders. Onboarding Checklist is maintained by HR Operations.

## Policy requirement
HR creates records five business days before start; IT provisions accounts; Operations issues badge on day one.

## Implementation steps
1. Log the request, business purpose, owner, and applicable region. 2. Verify the current policy version and eligibility. 3. Obtain the designated approval before execution. 4. Record the outcome and evidence in the department tracking system. 5. Escalate exceptions to HR Operations.

## Worked example
A staff member asks about onboarding checklist. The reviewer checks the rule above, validates employee scope, records approvals, and follows the related procedure in IT-003. Do not use superseded guidance for current requests.

## Controls and exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations. Confidential or Restricted material must only be retrieved for entitled roles. The owning team reviews the policy annually.

## Cross-department handoff
For overlapping questions, consult IT-003. If policies disagree, use the active version effective on the relevant date and escalate unresolved conflicts to the owners.
