# US PTO Policy

## Scope
This policy applies to US NovaTech staff and authorized stakeholders. US PTO Policy is maintained by HR Operations.

## Policy requirement
US employees earn 15 days in years 0–2, 20 days in years 3–5, and 25 days after five years.

## Implementation steps
1. Log the request, business purpose, owner, and applicable region. 2. Verify the current policy version and eligibility. 3. Obtain the designated approval before execution. 4. Record the outcome and evidence in the department tracking system. 5. Escalate exceptions to HR Operations.

## Worked example
A staff member asks about us pto policy. The reviewer checks the rule above, validates employee scope, records approvals, and follows the related procedure in IT-003. Do not use superseded guidance for current requests.

## Controls and exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from HR Operations. Confidential or Restricted material must only be retrieved for entitled roles. The owning team reviews the policy annually.
