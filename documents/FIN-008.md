# Customer Refunds

## Scope
This policy applies to US NovaTech staff and authorized stakeholders. Customer Refunds is maintained by FIN Operations.

## Policy requirement
Refunds up to $2,000 need CS director; larger require Controller and Legal.

## Implementation steps
1. Log the request, business purpose, owner, and applicable region. 2. Verify the current policy version and eligibility. 3. Obtain the designated approval before execution. 4. Record the outcome and evidence in the department tracking system. 5. Escalate exceptions to FIN Operations.

## Worked example
A staff member asks about customer refunds. The reviewer checks the rule above, validates employee scope, records approvals, and follows the related procedure in LEGAL-004. Do not use superseded guidance for current requests.

## Controls and exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from FIN Operations. Confidential or Restricted material must only be retrieved for entitled roles. The owning team reviews the policy annually.

## Cross-department handoff
For overlapping questions, consult LEGAL-004. If policies disagree, use the active version effective on the relevant date and escalate unresolved conflicts to the owners.
