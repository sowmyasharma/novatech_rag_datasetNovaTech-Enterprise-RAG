# Developer Setup

## Scope
This policy applies to Global NovaTech staff and authorized stakeholders. Developer Setup is maintained by ENG Operations.

## Policy requirement
No production data on laptops without de-identification and approval.

## Implementation steps
1. Log the request, business purpose, owner, and applicable region. 2. Verify the current policy version and eligibility. 3. Obtain the designated approval before execution. 4. Record the outcome and evidence in the department tracking system. 5. Escalate exceptions to ENG Operations.

## Worked example
A staff member asks about developer setup. The reviewer checks the rule above, validates employee scope, records approvals, and follows the related procedure in PROD-005. Do not use superseded guidance for current requests.

## Controls and exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from ENG Operations. Confidential or Restricted material must only be retrieved for entitled roles. The owning team reviews the policy annually.

## Cross-department handoff
For overlapping questions, consult PROD-005. If policies disagree, use the active version effective on the relevant date and escalate unresolved conflicts to the owners.
